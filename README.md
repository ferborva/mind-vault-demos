# Demos

Playable prototypes. Early, rough, and meant to be tried rather than admired.

## Memory Tricks

**Four memory tricks you actually keep** — and six levels of each, so it keeps getting harder
as you get better.

- 🎬 **Silly Film** — remember a list by joining things into a ridiculous picture. From level 4
  you have to give them back *in order*.
- 🏠 **Memory Palace** — build your own route through your home, then hide things along it.
  The route grows as you improve.
- 🙂 **Name Game** — never forget a name again.
- 🔒 **Number Lock** — hold numbers that won't stay put. Break them up, then turn the digits into
  things you can picture. Round 3 lets you choose where to split — that part is your call, and
  there's no right answer.

**🧠 Still got it?** — anything you learn comes back **about a day later** to see if it stayed.
That gap is the whole point: remembering something a day on is what actually counts, and *trying*
to remember is what makes it stick. It's an offer, never a demand — "Later" costs nothing and a
month away costs nothing either.

**📈 How you're doing** — a picture of you getting better. Every day you play is a dot; the line
is your best ever, so it only goes up. The dots can go down, and they're supposed to be able to:
a chart that only ever rises isn't telling you anything.

Get the first three tricks to level 3 and **The Grand Tour** opens: all three back to back, one
score. Eleven badges to find along the way.

Five minutes a go. **No typing anywhere.** Nothing is stored or sent — your scores live only in
your own browser, and clearing your browser data clears them.

**▶️ [Play it](https://ferborva.github.io/mind-vault-demos/memory/)**

## Six Moves

A ten-minute game about seeing a problem a second way. It teaches six named reframing
moves, then asks you to use them. You write your own version before you're shown another
one, and nothing you write is scored or stored.

Aimed at teenagers and adults — **it was tested on younger children and was too abstract
for them.** That's what prototypes are for.

**▶️ [Play it](https://ferborva.github.io/mind-vault-demos/)**
